Golana

The FM-1 as a single, deep analog-style synthesizer: two oscillators, five filters, two envelopes, two LFOs, a patch bay, an arpeggiator and a step sequencer. Every page draws what it does.

What's new in 1.3

2026-10-26

  • OSC 1's CTRL 1 and CTRL 2 say what they do: their names change with the WAVE (SAW: OCTAVE, OCT LFO; PULSE: WIDTH, PWM; TRI: FOLD, FOLD LFO; SINE: X-MOD, XMOD LFO), PULSE's WIDTH shows its duty in %, the drawing shows faintly how far CTRL 2 (LFO 1) moves the wave, and the page's fourth card is LFO 1's rate.
  • HOME's scope, quicker and with a trail: redrawn every frame with lines only, as Felucca's. A new view, TRAIL (the default): the wave now, the last ones behind it in the palette's second colour (NIGHT: orange and green), about 30 ms of sound (two or three cycles of a bass). ORBIT and RIDGE leave a trail too; TRAIL takes RING's place.
  • New waves: OSC 1's MORPH (CTRL 1 from the saw to the square), OSC 2's MIX (a saw and a square at once), and four new SUB types: -1 SAW, -2 SAW, -1 MIX, -2 MIX.
  • Cleaner oscillators: the wavefolders (FOLD, TRI's fold) without most of their aliasing (ADAA), SINE's cross modulation a little shallower high up (in tune, no whistling), and every note starts its oscillators from its own point of the wave, as an analog synth's (the attacks a little alive, no longer all alike).
  • Mod wheel (MIDI CC1): a vibrato on its own, or, routed in the PATCH, the sources MODW, MW*LFO1, MW*LFO2 (an LFO as deep as the wheel) and AFTER (aftertouch).
  • SCALE LOCK (SCL › SCALE): every key and MIDI note in the scale you choose (16 scales, any key).
  • CHORD (SCL › CHORD): a key or a MIDI note plays a chord: the scale's own triad or seventh, or a fixed shape (MAJ, MIN, DOM7, MAJ7, MIN7, SUS4, POW), in five voicings.
  • SUPERSAW, a new factory sound in program 25 (a program you wrote there stays).

A synth you can read

Twelve buttons open the sections, as on the classic microKORG: EDIT the oscillators, SCL the filter, ENV the envelopes, LFO the LFOs and the patch bay, FX the effects. KNOB 1–4 always set the four cards on the screen, ALGORITHM is always the cutoff.

What's inside

A single voice architecture, explored in depth, with 32 programs.

Two oscillators and a sub

OSC 1: SAW with an octave-up blend, PULSE with width, TRI with a wavefolder, SINE with cross modulation. OSC 2: SAW, SQR or TRI with RING, SYNC or both, semitone and tune. A SUB oscillator one or two octaves down (square, sine, saw or a mix). New in 1.3: OSC 1's MORPH, from the saw to the square, OSC 2's MIX, and cleaner wavefolders.

DRIFT

New in 1.2: analog age. Every note's oscillators a little out of tune, each its own way, slowly wandering, its filter a little off. From a touch of life to a worn-out tape.

Five filters

LPF24, LPF12, BPF, HPF and MS20, a driven 12 dB low-pass whose resonance screams. A FOLD stage before it, key tracking and its own envelope.

Envelopes and LFOs

FILTER EG and AMP EG; two LFOs (SAW, SQR, TRI, SIN, S&H) with key sync and tempo sync. Four patch cords route any of them to pitch, cutoff, amp and more.

Unison and glide

POLY, MONO, LEGATO and UNISON: four voices, or eight detuned oscillators in unison. DETUNE, PORTA, EG RESET and SPREAD across the stereo field.

Scale, chords, mod wheel

New in 1.3: SCALE LOCK keeps every key and MIDI note in one of 16 scales; CHORD plays the scale's triads and sevenths, or fixed shapes, from one key; a MIDI keyboard's mod wheel is a vibrato or a source in the patch.

Arpeggiator and sequencer

Ten arp types (ORDER, CONVERGE, PEDAL and DOUBLE new in 1.1) over 1–4 octaves with latch, swing and steps on/off; a 16-step sequencer with notes, ties, accents and slides, saved with the program.

Effects

MOD FX (chorus, flanger, ensemble, phaser, or METAL distortion), a stereo delay in time with the tempo and a reverb, in three orders.

Pages that draw

Each page draws its shape from the values: the waves, the filter's curve, the envelopes, the LFOs running. HOME draws the sound playing every frame, with a trail in a second colour: TRAIL, ORBIT or RIDGE.

32 programs

25 factory sounds (FAT BASS, ACID LINE, SYNC PAD, 80S BRASS, SUB BASS, WORN TAPE, AGED PAD; new in 1.3: SUPERSAW…) and seven free slots. MIDI Program Change picks them.

Four palettes

NIGHT, CRT, PAPER and NEON. And a three-second power-on with the eighties' stripes.

Sounds

Eight short pieces, each one factory program played as the FM-1 plays it: one synth, its four voices, its arpeggiator, delay and reverb. No effects added afterwards.

Safe to try

Custom firmware should never be a one-way door.

Install from the browser

Chrome or Edge, a USB data cable, two minutes. The installer checks the package before writing and asks the FM-1 what it runs afterwards. An interrupted install resumes.

USB rescue

Hold OCT− while switching on (or let two starts fail) and the FM-1 starts a small rescue mode with only USB, ready for a new install. The boot code itself is never written.

Back to official

The installer can put M-VAVE's official V15 firmware back at any time, after saving your music to a file.
